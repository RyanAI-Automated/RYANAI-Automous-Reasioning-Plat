# Grill me
Clarify outcome, audience, constraints, success criteria, risks and trade-offs. Ask one consolidated, prioritized interview rather than a stream of tiny questions. Reuse answers from context. Stop when material choices are resolved; do not over-interview routine details or treat silence as consent for high-risk actions.
