# Wayfinder
Use for initiatives too large for one session. Map destination, workstreams, dependencies, decision gates, owners, acceptance evidence and risks. Resolve blocking decisions in dependency order and preserve a durable status map. Do not turn the map into an open-ended planning exercise; each step must reduce uncertainty or deliver a verifiable increment.
